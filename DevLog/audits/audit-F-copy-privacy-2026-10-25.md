## Verdict

**FAIL for copy; PASS with one required fix for privacy.**

- Copy: 855 findings (36 high, 445 med, 374 low). Rules 1-5 (filler, process labels, tone): 622; rule 6 (parity): 173; rule 7 (false or contradictory): 60. Only 22 of 157 pages are clean. The bulk is not prose filler but **process text leaking into the page**: research-note ids, figure ids, lane names, agent notes ("to be checked by PHY", "not fetched", "search budgets exhausted", "via snippet", "returned 403"), and raw figure-spec text on `/lab/`. Banned-word filler proper is small (about 60 items, mostly "explorer").
- False site state is the most visible defect: stale `built: false` flags in `src/lib/content/site.ts` make the landing page, the footer wafer map on every page, `/crystal/` and `/light/` say that built pages are "not built" or "hold no data".
- Privacy: no absolute local paths, e-mail addresses, tokens, lab-internal links, employer references, or private-project references on any page, in `src/`, `content/`, `data/`, `static/`, `README.md` or `research/`. One required fix in `DevLog/` (P-1): a build rule names three private repositories and a home-directory folder named after the author's employer.
- No emoji anywhere in page text.
