LiNbO3 · trigonal · point group 3m · space group R3c

Lithium niobate

A trigonal ferroelectric oxide whose refractive index changes linearly with an applied electric field (Pockels effect) and whose second-order susceptibility χ(2) is large. Ti-indiffused bulk waveguides (1974) became the standard fiber-optic modulator; since 2017, sub-micron films on insulator (TFLN) carry the same physics on chips.
