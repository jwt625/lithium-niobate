L1 Phenomenon

bulk; MZM li2026aa

A field E changes the optical impermeability 1/n2 of a non-centrosymmetric crystal linearly. With field and optical polarization both along the polar axis c (= z), the extraordinary index shifts by

Δne=−12ne3r33Ez,Δφ=2πλΔneL(1)

Sign: a positive r33Ez lowers ne. A phase shift of π needs E·L = λ / (ne3 r33) for a uniform field that fills the mode.

L2 The r tensor

point group 3m

Index ellipsoid and its first-order perturbation, contracted index I: 1 = xx, 2 = yy, 3 = zz, 4 = yz, 5 = xz, 6 = xy; j runs over the field components.

∑i,j(1n2)ijxixj=1,Δ(1n2)I=∑j=13rIjEj,δnk=−12nk3Δ(1n2)kk(2)

Point group 3m, x perpendicular to a mirror plane, z along c: four independent coefficients. The last relation holds for polarization along a principal axis k; off-diagonal terms (I = 4, 5, 6) rotate the principal axes instead.
